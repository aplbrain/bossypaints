## Merging

To merge, pull out the merge/split panel with the left-side caret, or by tapping <kbd>m</kbd>:

<img width="1769" height="1040" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3a47f5bf-95bc-4a97-b8b9-bfe88b9230c0" />

Click "merge" and select the segments that belong together:

<img width="1144" height="944" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a7c9b35c-aecb-4729-be57-dca4f938b181" />

Applying the merge puts those segment IDs into a shared ID (which inherits the _lowest_ segmentation ID of the original set):

<img width="1191" height="966" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ca453c3c-7f50-4277-b798-a7d191f41920" />

Hitting "unmerge" pulls a particular segment ID out of the merge set:

<img width="372" height="333" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c5c2197f-fb16-4710-9797-0a1b59fe6f88" />

## Splitting

To split, pull out the merge/split panel with the left-side caret, or by tapping <kbd>m</kbd>:

<img width="1071" height="699" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/995b1f57-4b9e-4bd1-9497-133665c1516a" />

Click the "split" button, and begin placing "red" seeds, which correspond to the first of the two split bodies:

<img width="1069" height="697" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/168ec085-84c9-4fd6-ade7-62dfe076b6b8" />

Then click "Blue" and place seeds that correspond to the second. You can click points to delete them.

<img width="1067" height="720" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6c99e630-9fa3-4dec-83f4-f7d87c3c2f9c" />

Once you're happy with your split preview (don't forget you can both place points and preview the split on multiple z slices!), you can hit "Apply" to create a new segment ID for the blue body. This ID will automatically be assigned to the largest unassigned ID in your scene.

<img width="1071" height="683" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1abdb63e-7737-4a2c-bb6f-24e8532c1d79" />
